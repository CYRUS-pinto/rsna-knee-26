3.1 Volumetric Normalization & Fast I/O PipelineTo resolve scanner heterogeneity across international imaging centers, all incoming DICOM series are mapped to a canonical 5-slot physical volume tensor:$$\mathbf{V}_{\text{study}} \in \mathbb{R}^{96 \times 384 \times 384}$$+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5-SLOT PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION (Total = 96 Slices Native / 64 Slices Compact)       |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Slot 1: Sagittal Fluid-Sensitive (FS)      | 26 slices (native) | 18 slices (comp) |
| Slot 2: Sagittal Non-Fluid-Sensitive (T1)  | 22 slices (native) | 14 slices (comp) |
| Slot 3: Coronal Fluid-Sensitive (FS)       | 18 slices (native) | 12 slices (comp) |
| Slot 4: Coronal Non-Fluid-Sensitive        | 12 slices (native) |  8 slices (comp) |
| Slot 5: Axial (All Modalities)             | 18 slices (native) | 12 slices (comp) |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
3D Plane Normal Ordering: Evaluates directional cosines from tag (0028, 0030) ImageOrientationPatient $[r_x, r_y, r_z, c_x, c_y, c_z]$. Slices are sorted along the slice normal $n = r \times c$ via depth projection $d = \text{IPP} \cdot n$:$$n = r \times c = \begin{bmatrix} r_y c_z - r_z c_y \\ r_z c_x - r_x c_z \\ r_x c_y - r_y c_x \end{bmatrix}, \quad d_i = \mathbf{p}_i \cdot n$$Metric Physical Cropping: Uses in-plane spacing $(s_y, s_x)$ from tag (0028, 0030) PixelSpacing:$$\text{CropPixels}_y = \text{round}\left(\frac{140.0\text{ mm}}{\max(s_y, 10^{-3})}\right), \quad \text{CropPixels}_x = \text{round}\left(\frac{140.0\text{ mm}}{\max(s_x, 10^{-3})}\right)$$Wide-Span Quantile Slicing ($2\%\text{ to }98\%$): Samples linearly across $[0.02 \cdot N_s, 0.98 \cdot N_s - 1]$, capturing collateral ligaments and peripheral meniscal horns.2.5D Triplet Windowing: Stacks 3 physically adjacent slices into RGB-channel inputs $(z-1, z, z+1)$, yielding $K = 94$ overlapping evaluation windows.3.2 Multi-Scale Raptor CoAtNet Backbone (384px + 224px)The visual foundation is anchored by two CoAtNet models (coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384 and coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_224) pretrained on ImageNet-12k and fine-tuned on the expanded OAI-augmented knee corpus:Scale 1 (Primary, 65% Raptor Weight): Full $140\text{ mm}$ bounding box resampled to $384 \times 384$.Scale 2 (Focal Context, 35% Raptor Weight): Centered $0.80$ crop of the $140\text{ mm}$ field resampled to $224 \times 224$.Per-Finding Attention Head: Avoids uniform global pooling by assigning an independent projection query $W_c \in \mathbb{R}^{256 \times 1}$ to each pathology:$$a_{k, c} = \frac{\exp\left(W_c^T \tanh(V h_k)\right)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \exp\left(W_c^T \tanh(V h_j)\right)}, \quad z_c = \sum_{k=1}^K a_{k, c} h_k \cdot \text{clsW}_c + \text{clsb}_c$$This allows focal acute pathologies (ACL tears localized to 2 sagittal slices) to attend to their pathognomonic slices, while diffuse conditions (Osteoarthritis) aggregate across coronal cartilage compartments.Mirror Test-Time Augmentation (TTA): Executes a plain forward pass and a horizontally mirrored forward pass on the 384px model, averaging probabilities in logit space.3.3 Heliosli ConvNeXt-Large R6 4-Fold BundleThe second engine provides orthogonal architectural inductive bias via convnext_large.fb_in22k_ft_in1k_384 managed by probe_dual.py:Bilateral Coronal & Centered Locals: Generates localized $100\text{ mm}$ bounding crops focusing specifically on the medial and lateral joint compartments.Morphological D2 Fluid Volumetry: Extracts the lowest-background fluid-sensitive sagittal series, applies thresholding at the 99th intensity percentile ($P_{99} \ge 229$), executes morphological opening with an elliptical structuring element ($r = 1.5\text{ mm} / s_x$), and quantifies absolute fluid volume in milliliters:$$V_{\text{fluid}} = \sum_{z=1}^{N_z} \left(\sum_{x, y} M_{\text{open}}(x, y, z) \cdot s_x s_y\right) \cdot \frac{\Delta z}{1000} \quad [\text{mL}]$$Anatomical Permutation Flip TTA: Evaluates native and mirrored volumes, re-indexing symmetric bilateral findings with permutation operator $P$:$$P = (\text{Medial Meniscus} \leftrightarrow \text{Lateral Meniscus}) \times (\text{Medial OA} \leftrightarrow \text{Lateral OA})$$3.4 2.5D Fast Reader & Specialist MIL EnginesA lightweight 2.5D ConvNeXt reader (_reader_sub.csv) provides additional feature diversity. In accordance with the Versia_7 benchmark, this engine is blended with conservative, finding-specific weights ($0.003$ to $0.035$, mean $0.016$) rather than uniform blending ($0.15$), preventing dilution of the deep OAI-pretrained representations.4. Calibration Mathematics & Ensembling Theory4.1 Empirical Percentile Rank StandardizationBecause the evaluation metric is Macro Mean ROC-AUC, absolute probability calibration is irrelevant; only relative ordering within each pathology column determines performance. Direct linear averaging of raw sigmoid outputs $\sigma(z_1)$ and $\sigma(z_2)$ creates severe distortion because the steeper model dominates the rank ordering.We map raw model scores to empirical percentiles over the test cohort:$$\text{RankPct}(x_i) = \frac{\text{rank}(x_i)}{N} \in [1/N, 1.0]$$<p align="center">

For acute focal lesions (ACL, Meniscus, Fracture), high-confidence concordant detections between independent backbones represent verified pathology that should be amplified non-linearly without artificial heuristic thresholding.We formulate the Generalized Power-Mean:$$M_p(x, y; w) = \left( w \cdot x^p + (1 - w) \cdot y^p \right)^{1/p}$$Focal Acute Pathologies ($p = 1.02\text{ to }1.06$, $p=1.40$ in V20): Amplifies high-percentile signals smoothly without heuristic threshold clipping.Diffuse Degenerative Pathologies ($p = 1.00$): Preserves linear rank blending on Osteoarthritis and Effusion.4.3 Probit Normal Transformation (Acklam Rational Approximation)Uniform percentile ranks compress the extreme tails: the numerical difference between the 99th and 99.9th percentile is identical to the difference between the 50th and 50.9th percentile ($0.009$). In diagnostic radiology, extreme upper-tail conviction must be preserved.<p align="center">
<img src="assets/figure3_power_probit_calibration.png" alt="Power-Mean Response Curves and Probit Tail Calibration" width="100%"/>
</p>Using Acklam's rational approximation for the Inverse Normal Cumulative Distribution Function $\Phi^{-1}(p)$, uniform ranks are mapped to Gaussian $z$-scores:$$z_i = \Phi^{-1}\left( \text{clip}\left( r_i \cdot \frac{N - 1}{N} + \frac{0.5}{N}, 10^{-4}, 1 - 10^{-4} \right) \right)$$Ensembling is performed in Gaussian space and projected back via $\Phi(z)$, enabling extreme tail predictions to resolve near-ties cleanly:$$z_{\text{blend}} = \sum_{m} w_m \Phi^{-1}(r_m), \quad \hat{s}_{\text{probit}} = \Phi(z_{\text{blend}}) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ 1 + \text{erf}\left(\frac{z_{\text{blend}}}{\sqrt{2}}\right) \right]$$4.4 12x12 Orthopaedic Biomechanical Kinetic Coupling MatrixMusculoskeletal knee injuries follow well-established kinetic trauma pathways:[Acute Trauma Kinetic Cascade]

Forensic Post-Mortem & Diagnostic Case StudiesCase 1: The V50 Zero-Score Catastrophe (LB 0.4910)Root Cause: An automated script runner replaced the production DICOM pipeline with an offline lookup inside all_vols.npy. However, all_vols.npy contains strictly the 4,407 training studies. When executed against hidden test studies, lookup queries returned empty arrays, triggering fail-soft fallback code that populated predictions with np.random.beta(0.5, 2.0) noise.Engineering Remedy: Hidden test studies must stream and decode directly from test_series/<StudyInstanceUID>/<SeriesInstanceUID>/*.dcm. The AST Pre-Flight Guardrail now explicitly prohibits training corpus lookups on evaluation studies.Case 2: The Triple Rank-Quantization Cliff (0.9580 to 0.9570)Root Cause: In Candidate V53/V56, rank_matrix() was invoked sequentially across three stages: once after base anchor generation, once after auxiliary branch blending, and once inside apply_biomechanical_coupling(). On small batches, ranking snaps smooth continuous predictions to discrete fractions ($0.3333, 0.6667, 1.0000$), artificially amplifying subtle $0.0333$ margin differences into massive $0.3333$ cliffs.Engineering Remedy: Replaced discrete step ranking with continuous convex blending and Probit Gaussian tail calibration ($\Phi^{-1}(p)$), preserving exact continuous margin separations.Case 3: Auxiliary 16-Window Feature Dilution (0.9580 to 0.9530)Root Cause: Ensembling 16-window non-OAI auxiliary models (all_vols.npy) at $20\%\text{ to }50\%$ weights steadily eroded leaderboard scores ($50\% \to 0.953, 35\% \to 0.954, 20\% \to 0.955, 0\% \to 0.958$). The 16-window models lacked the through-plane spatial resolution of 528-window localized OAI models.Engineering Remedy: Prioritized 528-window OAI models; restricted auxiliary branches to conservative tie-breaking weights ($0.003$ to $0.035$, mean $0.016$) on verified high-performing targets.Case 4: CPU Thread Contention in 4-vCPU Virtual Machines (V58)Root Cause: Raising ThreadPoolExecutor from 6 to 12 workers slowed execution by $38\%$ ($660.9\text{ s}$ vs $551.7\text{ s}$) due to GIL thrashing and thread context switching on Kaggle's 4-vCPU allocation.Engineering Remedy: Locked thread pool concurrency strictly to 6 workers for all asynchronous I/O and preprocessing tasks.7.
